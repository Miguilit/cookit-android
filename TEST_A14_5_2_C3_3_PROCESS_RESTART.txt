C3.3-B test
1. Install A14.5.2 over A14.5.1 (no uninstall).
2. Confirm runtime_id and Fiscal Agent credentials survived.
3. Start auto once and confirm auto=ON.
4. Create a test_only cloud job with metadata.scenario=lost_response_once.
5. Wait until Cloud shows claimed / attempts=1 and no submitted_at.
6. Android Settings > Apps > Cookit > Force stop.
7. Wait until the cloud claim lease has expired, then manually launch Cookit.
8. Do not press Start auto. Expected: auto returns ON by itself after bootstrap.
9. Expected final Cloud state: attempts=2, fiscalized, exactly one receipt, duplicate=true.
10. fiscal_profiles.enabled must remain 0.

Note: Android force-stop intentionally prevents the package from restarting itself until the user
launches the app again. This test validates durable resume after that relaunch.
